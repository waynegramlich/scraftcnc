# 2.3 Mounting

How the stock is held on the machine (draft by Claude from the user's
description, 2026-10-27; the user's to revise).

* Unlike tools (all round shanks), mounting hardware has many shapes.
  For vise based mounting, from the table up: the T-slotted table, a
  vise (usually) on it, parallels in the vise, sometimes a tooling
  plate on the parallels, often sacrificial material on that, then
  the stock.  There is much else besides vises (clamps, hold downs,
  fixtures).
* Each piece needs its sizes, so heights add up (where the stock's top
  and bottom are) and `z_safe` and tool reach can be checked.
* Uses waiting on it, from `T.cuwp~2`: bottom chamfers with the double
  angle cutter (the cutter goes below the part, over sacrificial
  material); holding the part with tooling holes screwed to a tooling
  plate's grid (the user picks the holes); tabs in two phases (phase
  1 jumps over the phase 1 tabs where the hold downs are; phase 2
  avoids the phase 2 hold downs and removes the phase 1 tabs).
